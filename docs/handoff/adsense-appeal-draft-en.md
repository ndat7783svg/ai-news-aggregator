Publisher ID: pub-3734960989036733
Site: bainews.site

Subject: Appeal — Account disabled for "related to a disabled account", believe this is a false
positive due to shared/CGNAT IP address

Hello,

I am writing to appeal the disabling of my AdSense account (pub-3734960989036733) for the site
bainews.site. The notice states the account is "related to a disabled account."

I want to state clearly: **this is the first AdSense, AdMob, or Ad Manager account I have ever
created.** I have never owned, managed, or had access to any other Google Ads/AdSense account,
disabled or otherwise, before this one.

My best explanation for the "related account" flag is that I access the internet through a
mobile ISP in Vietnam that uses Carrier-Grade NAT (CGNAT), meaning my public IP address is shared
with many other unrelated subscribers. It is possible that another user sharing the same IP
address at some point had an AdSense account that was disabled for policy violations, and this
created an incorrect association with my account.

bainews.site is a legitimate news aggregation and summarization site (AI news, bilingual
Vietnamese/English). It does not host or republish full articles — it summarizes news in our own
words and always links back to the original source. I have not engaged in any invalid traffic,
click fraud, or policy-violating behavior.

I would appreciate it if you could review my account again. I am happy to provide any additional
information or verification needed to confirm my identity and the legitimacy of this account.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Sincerely,
[Tên đầy đủ của bạn]
